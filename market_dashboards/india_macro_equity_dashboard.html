Key structural insights

  • 2003–07 supercycle — GDP growth 14–16%, EPS growth 25–35%, Nifty delivered 40–70% annual returns driven by a PE re-rating from ~12x to ~22x
  • 2008 GFC — EPS growth collapsed to -15%, Nifty fell ~52%; GDP remained positive (8.5%) — classic decoupling of financial markets from real economy
  • 2010–16 consolidation — nominal GDP moderated to 10–12%, EPS growth was erratic (NPA cycle), index returns were muted (5–10% CAGR)
  • 2020 COVID — GDP contracted 6%, EPS fell sharply; yet Nifty recovered 15% by year-end on massive liquidity injection
  • 2021–24 recovery — GDP rebounded to 14–17% nominal growth, EPS grew 20–28%, Nifty compounded at 18–20% — strongest post-reform cycle
  • Long-run insight — EPS growth has outpaced nominal GDP by ~1.8% CAGR, reflecting mix shift toward higher-margin sectors (IT, financials, consumer)

Note: Pre-2004 Nifty EPS is estimated from Prowess/CMIE aggregates. GDP figures from MoSPI (2011-12 base). Returns are price returns; total returns (with dividends) would add ~1.5% CAGR. 2025-26 data are IMF/NSO advance estimates.
