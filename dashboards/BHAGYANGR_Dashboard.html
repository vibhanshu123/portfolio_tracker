Investment Thesis

Bhagyanagar India Limited delivered its best year in 40 years in FY26: revenue crossed ₹2,000 Cr for the first time, operational EBITDA crossed ₹100 Cr, and PAT crossed ₹50 Cr — all firsts. The pivot from commodity copper to value-added products (VAP 59% of revenue, up from ~35% in FY22) is structural and measurable — EBITDA/kg rose from ₹14 (FY22) to ₹43 (FY26 avg) to ₹62 (Q4 FY26). ROCE leapt from 8% to 21% in a single year. The NCLT demerger hearing (9 June 2026) is the next binary catalyst: approval separates high-growth copper (Tieramet Ltd) from Hyderabad land assets (BIL). However, the stock has already delivered 285% in 12 months and now trades at its 52-week high (₹305/₹306). Base-case fair value is ₹310–340, implying minimal near-term upside. The bear case (₹120) reflects ₹169 Cr in contingent liabilities (66% of equity), ongoing management churn (MD, CFO, CS all changed in 18 months), and potential margin reversion. We rate WATCH: existing holders should stay; new investors should await NCLT outcome and a better entry below ₹260.

Business Overview

Bhagyanagar India Limited is a 40-year-old Hyderabad-based copper products manufacturer serving 500+ clients across auto, power, solar, and industrial sectors. The standalone entity holds wind power assets (6 MW) and owns 100% of Bhagyanagar Copper Pvt Ltd (BCPL) — the copper manufacturing WOS where all copper operations reside since Jan 2024 slump sale. A composite scheme of arrangement (Composite Scheme) is pending NCLT approval to split BCPL+BIL into Tieramet Limited (copper) and a real-estate-focused BIL.

Structural Demand Drivers
⚡ AI / Data Centers
Silver-plated bus bars for power distribution in hyperscaler data centers. BIL secured 106 MT at USD 1.33M (USD 12,500/MT — 3–4x standard pricing). India's data center capex: USD 5B+ through 2028.
☀ Solar Energy
India targets 50+ GW solar addition per year. Each GW requires significant copper ribbon volume. BIL positioned as domestic supplier vs imports. PV ribbon added to portfolio in 2023.
⚡ EV & Auto
EVs use 3–4x more copper than ICE vehicles. BIL supplies auto OEMs (Lucas TVS 35+ yrs) and EV charging infra bus bars. India EV penetration to accelerate through 2030.
⚡ Power Grid
India's power transmission capex: ₹3L+ Cr through FY30. Transformer windings, bus bars for switchgear. L&T, Toshiba, Medha Servo are direct customers.
🕒 Railways Electrification
Medha Servo is a key BIL client for railway traction motors. Indian Railways 100% electrification target drives copper demand for overhead wiring and traction equipment.
⚠ LME Copper Price Risk
Raw material is ~90% of cost. Price pass-through model protects revenue but compresses margins if LME spikes. 50–60% inventory hedged on MCX. Net forex exposure partially hedged.

Growth Trigger Cards
#1 — Silver-Plated Bus Bars (AI Data Centers) HIGH
Opportunity: AI/hyperscaler boom in India. BIL is the only Indian maker of bus bars up to 300mm. Initial order 106 MT at USD 1.33M (USD 12,500/MT = 3–4x standard pricing). Ongoing conversations for repeat/larger orders.
Timeline: Now — repeat orders FY27+. USD 5B+ data center investments in India by 2028.
Bear pushback: Data center capex can delay or shift overseas. First order is only 106 MT; scale-up unproven. USD pricing volatile.
#2 — Solar PV Interconnect Ribbons HIGH
Opportunity: India 50+ GW/year solar target. Each GW needs significant copper ribbon volume. BIL entered in 2023 as domestic supplier vs imports (China). Growing rapidly.
Timeline: FY26–FY29. Growing with India's solar build-out cycle.
Bear pushback: Chinese imports. Module manufacturers under margin pressure. Policy risk if solar PLI changes.
#3 — Capacity Expansion 35K → 50K MT MEDIUM
Opportunity: Current 35K MT (fully utilized at ~24K MT sold = 69% util). 50K MT target for FY27-28 would enable ₹3,500–4,000 Cr revenue at current realizations.
Timeline: FY27-28. Capex to be announced. Management guided ₹5,000 Cr by FY28-29.
Bear pushback: Capex requires debt. Working capital grows proportionally. No formal announcement yet.
#4 — Demerger Real Estate Value Unlock MEDIUM
Opportunity: 3 Hyderabad industrial land parcels. Upal: 16L sq ft FSI development potential. Book value ₹200–300 Cr at circle rates; management says market value "significantly higher." Post-demerger BIL = pure real estate play.
Timeline: NCLT hearing 9 June 2026. Actual monetization likely FY27-28.
Bear pushback: NCLT can delay further. Real estate development timelines unpredictable. No developer announced yet.
#5 — Credit Rating Upgrade: BBB+ → A- OPTIONALITY
Opportunity: Current BCPL rating BBB+. Target A- by end FY27. A- unlocks borrowing cost below 8% (vs 8.5–9% now). At ₹259 Cr debt, 100bps rate reduction = ~₹2.6 Cr interest saving = direct PAT upside.
Timeline: FY27 end if margins sustain at 5%+.
Bear pushback: Contingent liabilities (₹169 Cr) may delay rating agencies. Management churn is negative for rating agencies.
#6 — VAP Mix Expansion: 59% → 70%+ OPTIONALITY
Opportunity: VAP volume grew +46.7% YoY in FY26. Each 1% shift from commodity to VAP adds ~₹1.5–2 Cr to EBITDA. Target mix 70%+ would push margins toward 5.5–6%.
Timeline: Gradual — FY27-FY29. Depends on product qualification cycles at OEMs/utilities.
Bear pushback: Q1 FY27 mix could regress if commodity orders surge. VAP capacity is ceiling-constrained without 50K MT expansion.

Valuation Verdict
Valuation: FAIRLY VALUED

BIL trades at 19.3x trailing P/E — cheap vs Hindustan Copper (82x) and Mardia (484x), but those peers have structurally higher margins (37.9% vs BIL's 4.5%). BIL's EV/EBITDA of 11.1x is the cheapest in the peer group and arguably the right metric for a capital-light rerating story. The bull case requires margins sustaining 5%+ and the demerger unlocking ₹100–200 Cr in land value. At current P/E of 19x, the market is pricing in one more year of growth at FY26's pace. Any margin disappointment (e.g., LME spike, product mix reversal) would compress P/E and create downside to ₹180–220.
